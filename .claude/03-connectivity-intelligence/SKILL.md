# AERIVA Connectivity Intelligence Skill
Turn measured network conditions into honest recommendations.

## Measurements
Use only metrics actually available on the target Android version/network, such as transport, network state, signal-related information where available, latency, jitter, packet loss, throughput, DNS/connectivity checks, transitions, timestamps, and confidence.

Document scoring models. Do not manufacture metrics.

## Best-spot intelligence
AERIVA only knows locations it has actually sampled or has privacy-preserving community evidence for. Show freshness and confidence. Do not continuously track users merely to build maps.

Example: "Better connection observed near the window. Based on 6 recent measurements."

Never promise an unmeasured location, perfect signal, guaranteed speed, or guaranteed ping.

## Activity profiles
Support gaming, video calls, streaming, downloads, and general use where meaningful. Do not force every activity into one generic score.

## Network transitions
Represent Wi-Fi/mobile changes, loss of validated internet, degradation, and recovery without excessive switching recommendations.

## Technical truth
AERIVA cannot physically amplify carrier radio signal and cannot transparently optimize arbitrary encrypted traffic without a supported technical path.
