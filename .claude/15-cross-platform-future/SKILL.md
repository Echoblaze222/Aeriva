# AERIVA Cross Platform Strategy Skill
Android is the current primary platform.

Do not compromise Android quality for hypothetical future platforms.

Keep domain concepts portable where useful: connectivity measurements, data usage, budgets, recommendations, confidence, freshness, community aggregation, and synchronization.

Keep Android-specific implementations isolated: ConnectivityManager, VpnService, network statistics, background execution, permissions, and notification behavior.

Use interfaces only when there is a real boundary or test benefit. Avoid speculative abstractions.
