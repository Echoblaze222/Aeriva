# AERIVA Community Data Quality Skill
Community intelligence must be trustworthy.

Consider measurement age, contributor diversity, spatial aggregation, repeated identical values, impossible values, outliers, device/network inconsistencies, and measurement frequency.

Document scoring and thresholds. Prefer transparent deterministic rules initially.

Community data must decay in relevance over time. Rate-limit contributions and prevent one contributor from dominating an area.

Use confidence-aware language such as "Based on recent measurements", "Limited evidence", or "No recent measurements".
